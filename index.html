About the Weaver

Alemnesh turned her deep love for authentic Ethiopian textiles into a thriving, modern fashion venture. When she’s not busy curating vibrant threads and playing with fresh silhouettes, she’s building a brand that proves traditional handlooms belong in modern everyday life—not just stored away for special occasions.

For her, weaving isn't just business—it’s pure energy, self-expression, and storytelling through fabric.

Have a design vision or want to bring a custom idea to life? Alemnesh is always excited to create something magic with you!
